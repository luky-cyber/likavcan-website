# Identity & Discovery Polish — v0.1.6

## Design intent

Likavcan.cz remains a professional identity hub, not a content portal. This release borrows trust and discovery patterns from the Bezpečná elektrika project without copying its service-oriented information architecture.

## Adopted patterns

1. Source/methodology transparency.
2. Explicit entity relationship explanation.
3. Controlled AI disclosure.
4. Editorial lastmod policy.
5. Human-useful vCard identity files.
6. Accessibility skip links.
7. Search/discovery crawler access while opting GPTBot out.

## Deliberately not adopted

- Full-site search overlay.
- Glossary, FAQ, podcast or service navigation.
- LocalBusiness/Service schema.
- Dark mode in this release.
- Empty article sections.
- Additional knowsAbout expansion.
