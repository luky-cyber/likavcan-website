Likavcan.cz v0.1.3 GA4 consent integration

Changed files:
- assets/js/main.js
- assets/css/components.css

Measurement ID:
- G-0F00N4385D

Behaviour:
1. Google Analytics is not loaded before consent.
2. "Accept all" stores consent and loads GA4.
3. "Essential only" and closing the banner keep GA4 disabled.
4. Saved consent is respected on later visits.
5. A multilingual Cookie settings button allows the visitor to reopen the banner and change the choice.
6. Google signals and advertising personalisation signals are disabled.

Testing:
- Open DevTools Console and run:
  localStorage.removeItem("likavcan_cookie_consent"); location.reload();
- Choose Essential only and confirm no request to googletagmanager.com is made.
- Clear consent again, choose Accept all, and confirm a request to googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-0F00N4385D is made.
