01 · Context

Why the project exists

Electrical safety is often presented either too technically or too simplistically. The project aims to work from actual conditions, measurement and documentation while explaining technical context without pretending certainty where evidence is incomplete.

02 · Real-world problem

When documentation is missing, start with reality

In an older 1960s house without complete documentation, I am progressively mapping circuits, sub-distribution boards and the actual installation state. Photographs, measurements and notes are assembled into a clearer digital map.

This is a documentation and analysis process; the digital map does not replace an inspection, project documentation or professional assessment.

03 · Digital engineering workflow

Humans measure and verify; tools help organise

AI and QElectroTech are supporting tools. Responsibility for observation, measurement, interpretation and verification remains human.

1 · Observe and measure

Actual state, photographs, circuit identification and measurement inputs.

2 · Organise evidence

AI may help classify information, compare records and identify inconsistencies.

3 · QElectroTech

Topology and schematics are transferred into maintainable technical documentation.

4 · Human verification

Outputs are checked against the real installation and available professional sources.

05 · Boundaries

What the project does not replace

The digital map and QElectroTech documentation do not replace an inspection report, design documentation or professional assessment. AI helps organise information; measurement, interpretation and responsibility remain human.

Detailed technical articles, methodology and other outputs are published on the project’s original website.

Current status

The E2/A inspection-technician examination has been successfully completed and the certificate has been issued. Commercial inspection services have not yet launched.

07 · Working approach

How I work on the project

The project combines systematic measurement and observation, technical documentation, professional research and clear communication. I use digital tools, including AI, as support rather than a substitute for professional verification.
