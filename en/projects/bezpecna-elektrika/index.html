01 · Primary purpose

Service first, content second

The latest Bezpečná elektrika release is deliberately service-first: visitors should quickly understand that the core of the project is the planned inspection of electrical installations and equipment. Educational content supports customer orientation and professional trust rather than being the primary offer.

After launch, the planned scope focuses on family houses and apartments, lightning-protection systems on family houses, and selected electrical installations, equipment, appliances and extension leads in administrative premises.

02 · Inspection approach

The result is more than a measured value

The service concept follows inspection → testing and measurement → engineering evaluation → documented and clearly explained conclusion. This reflects the principle Measure. Document. Explain.

1 · Agree the scope

Define what is to be verified and what documentation is available.

2 · Inspect, test and measure

Collect the inputs required for the specific installation or equipment.

3 · Evaluate professionally

Measurements need context; a number alone is not an engineering conclusion.

4 · Document and explain

Findings should remain traceable and understandable within the inspection scope.

03 · Supporting engineering workflow

Humans measure and decide; digital tools organise

Photographs, measurements, notes, QElectroTech and AI support can help turn real-world observations into maintainable documentation. They do not replace professional inspection, physical measurement or the responsibility of the qualified inspection technician.

1 · Real condition

Observation, photographs, circuit identification and measurement inputs.

2 · Organise evidence

AI may help classify information and flag inconsistencies.

3 · Technical documentation

QElectroTech and supporting records help keep topology and evidence readable.

4 · Human verification

Interpretation and final responsibility remain with the qualified person.

05 · Evidence & limitations

Service-first positioning does not change the boundaries of claims

Public evidence consists of the Bezpečná elektrika website, the inspection-services page, methodology, technical outputs and the documented workflow. Likavcan.cz does not duplicate that content; it explains what the project demonstrates.

A digital map, QElectroTech documentation or AI support does not replace an inspection report, design documentation or professional assessment. The current presentation must also not imply that commercial inspection services have already launched.

06 · Qualification and commercial status

Planned services, transparent status

The E2A inspection-technician examination has been successfully completed and the certificate has been issued. Commercial inspection services have not yet launched. Likavcan.cz and Bezpečná elektrika should keep this status consistent.

07 · Proof of work

What the project demonstrates

Bezpečná elektrika demonstrates service-oriented engineering: identifying the customer need, systematic inspection and measurement, documentation, professional evaluation and clear communication. Technical content and digital tools support that service; they do not replace it or redefine the primary materials-engineering identity on Likavcan.cz.
