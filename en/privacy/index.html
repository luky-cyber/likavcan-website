Privacy & Analytics

Likavcan.cz uses essential technologies for core functionality and optional analytics only after visitor consent.

Controller and contact

The controller of personal data processed through Likavcan.cz is Ing. Lukáš Likavčan, PhD. Questions about the website or privacy can be directed through the public professional LinkedIn profile.

Essential technologies

Your browser stores the analytics choice locally under likavcan_cookie_consent. The record contains the selected mode, decision time and policy version. A new choice is requested after a material policy change or approximately after 12 months.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-0F00N4385D) loads only after explicit consent. The legal basis is consent and the purpose is basic aggregate understanding of visits and use of public pages. Advertising storage, Google Signals and ad personalisation remain disabled. Page location and referrer sent to analytics are minimised by removing the query string and fragment.

PurposeTraffic and website-use measurement
Legal basisVisitor consent
ActivationOnly after visitor consent
ProviderGoogle Analytics / Google
RetentionAccording to the current Google Analytics property setting; the data is not intended for permanent storage
WithdrawalAt any time through “Cookie settings”

Withdrawing consent

If you switch from analytics consent to “Essential only”, the website sets analytics storage to denied, activates the GA disable flag, removes accessible first-party GA cookies and reloads the page. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before consent was withdrawn.

Technical operation and recipients

The website is statically published through GitHub Pages. During technical operation, infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical data such as an IP address, request time, requested resource or browser information to the extent needed for secure and reliable operation. When analytics is enabled, Google Analytics is an additional recipient.

Some providers may also process data outside the European Economic Area; where applicable, the relevant GDPR transfer safeguards and the provider's terms apply.

Data subject rights

To the extent provided by the GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection where they apply to the specific processing. Analytics consent can be withdrawn at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority.

External links

The website links to third-party services such as ORCID, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, ResearchGate, Wikidata and Bezpečná elektrika. Once an external link is opened, data processing is governed by that service's own policies.
