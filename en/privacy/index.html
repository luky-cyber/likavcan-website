Privacy & Analytics

Likavcan.cz uses essential technologies for core functionality and optional analytics only after visitor consent.

Website operator

Likavcan.cz is the personal professional website of Lukáš Likavčan. Questions about the website or privacy can be directed through the public professional LinkedIn profile.

Essential technologies

Your browser stores the consent choice locally under likavcan_cookie_consent. It is used only to remember whether analytics is allowed or essential-only mode is selected.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-0F00N4385D) loads only after explicit consent. Advertising signals and ad personalisation are disabled. Analytics is used for basic understanding of visits and use of public pages.

PurposeTraffic and website-use measurement
ActivationOnly after visitor consent
ProviderGoogle Analytics / Google LLC
WithdrawalAt any time through “Cookie settings”

Withdrawing consent

If you switch from analytics consent to “Essential only”, the website sets analytics storage to denied, activates the GA disable flag, removes accessible first-party GA cookies and reloads the page. This returns the current visit to a state without active analytics.

External links

The website links to third-party services such as ORCID, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, ResearchGate, Wikidata and Bezpečná elektrika. Once an external link is opened, data processing is governed by that service's own policies.
