Sources and Digital Identity

How Likavcan.cz uses sources, verifies professional claims, maintains stable entities and uses AI as a supporting tool.

Source principle

Publicly traceable academic, professional and institutional sources are preferred. The website is an identity hub, not the sole evidence for a claim.

How claims are verified

Important claims follow a source → check → comparison → interpretation → publication workflow. When sources do not support a definitive conclusion, the text should state the uncertainty instead of hiding it behind confident wording.

Stable entities and relationships

Lukáš Likavčan and Peter Likavčan use stable Person identifiers. sameAs is reserved for profiles of the same person; external records about a person are represented through subjectOf or visible references. Bezpečná elektrika is a separate Project linked to Lukáš through founder.

Technical content methodology

Technical content distinguishes observation from interpretation, states relevant assumptions and limitations, and prefers primary or official sources where possible. A simulation or measurement result is not automatically an engineering conclusion without context.

AI as a supporting tool

AI may support research, structure, language review, comparison of source material and consistency checks. It does not replace measurement, professional sources, engineering assessment or human responsibility for published claims.

Updates and dates

Update dates reflect meaningful editorial or technical-content changes, not builds, CSS changes or internal releases. Sitemap lastmod values are therefore not changed mechanically for every deployment.

Discovery and crawling

OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User are allowed; GPTBot is blocked. llms.txt is a supplementary map, not a replacement for HTML, sitemaps, internal links or high-quality public content.

What this methodology does not do

Methodology does not create authority by itself. It does not replace qualifications, independent sources or real professional outputs. New projects, publications and credentials should be added only when concrete evidence exists.
