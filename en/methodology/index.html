Sources and working principles

A short note on sources, technical content and updates.

On this page

Sources

For professional and technical claims, we prefer original publications, academic databases and official sources. When the available sources do not support a clear conclusion, we state the uncertainty or limitation.

Technical content

We separate measurement, simulation and observation from the interpretation that follows. A result without context is not yet an engineering conclusion, so for important claims we also state relevant assumptions and limits.

Updates

We change an update date when the content changes in a meaningful editorial or technical way, not for every internal website change.
