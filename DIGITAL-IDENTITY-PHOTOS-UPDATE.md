# Likavcan.cz – digital identity photo update

This package extends the existing entity-identity version with consistent professional portraits.

## Added
- Professional portrait of Lukáš Likavčan in JPG and WebP.
- Professional portrait of Peter Likavčan in JPG and WebP.
- Visible portraits on Slovak, English and German profile hero sections.
- Portraits on the three language home-page profile cards.
- `og:image` / Twitter image metadata for all six person profile pages.
- `image` references in Person JSON-LD.
- `primaryImageOfPage` ImageObject on ProfilePage structured data.
- Responsive portrait styling.

## Files
- `assets/img/profiles/lukas-likavcan.jpg` / `.webp`
- `assets/img/profiles/peter-likavcan.jpg` / `.webp`

The images are intended to be the canonical professional portraits used consistently with external profiles such as LinkedIn.
